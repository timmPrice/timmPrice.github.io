I have become spoiled during my undergrad with the quality of both hardware and software I have access to on a daily basis. I’m fortunate enough to rarely have to worry about the amount of compute I have at my finger-tips. I’ve also spent an unfortunate amount of time obsessively optimizing the way I choose to use my computers, often to the surprise of others (especially my coworkers). Because people often ask me questions about my setup, this list is a very surface-level organization of the tools I choose to use so I have somewhere to point to when people get curious. As I write more posts, my hope is that more detail about everything listed below will be found in separate writings. “All will be explained in due time“ so to speak.

My laptop is my primary computer as of 2026-03-09.
HP Envy x360:

  • AMD Ryzen 7 5800U (16) @ 4.4 Ghz
  • Currently running Fedora with I3WM.

Desktop ·

My desktop (cordially known by myself as the “ship of Theseus“):

  • AMD Ryzen 5 5600 (12) @ 4.47 GHz
  • Radeon RX 5700xt, 8gb GDDR6 @ 1795 MHz
  • 32gb ddr4 @ 3200 Mhz
  • Running my proxmox home-lab.

Operating System ·

Windows has its place but not on any of my personal devices. I use Linux on every device I’ve paid for no exceptions. I currently, and have been for around two years, been daily driving Fedora Linux on both my laptop and desktop (before making it a server). I'm working on migrating to a new Linux distro and so far am trying out both Nix OS and Alpine virtually before committing to a switch. I use I3 Window Manager on my laptop and Gnome for gaming on my desktop.

Editor + Terminal ·

Most of my software choices are standard and can be found in my dotfiles on GitHub, but the standout picks I recommend are:

  • I use NeoVim as my primary code/text editor.
  • I take notes using Obsidian (especially during math classes; imagine org-mode without setting up emacs).
  • In my terminal I use the "friendly interactive shell" or fish.
